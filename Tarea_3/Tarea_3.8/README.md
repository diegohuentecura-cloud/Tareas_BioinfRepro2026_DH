### Conclusion
This exercise helped me understand how to perform a test in R to evaluate matrices while controlling for another variable. In this case, the analysis compared genetic distances with different environmental scenarios using 10,000 permutations.

This can be applied in population genetics and ecology to study whether genetic differences between populations are associated with environmental or geographic factors. It is relevant because it helps identify relationships between variables while considering the effect of another factor that could influence the results.
